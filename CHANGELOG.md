# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section is that version's release notes: the release
workflow publishes it as the GitHub Release text, which the Ragnarok Offline
app shows players under "What's new". Write it for players.

## 0.3.6
- Needs app 1.6.4 or newer, the first with the Druid job line.
- The Eden Group's gear now includes the Druid line. Administrator Michael
  gives Druids the intermediate Druid Axe or Mace and Karnos the advanced
  Karnos Mace or Two-Handed Axe; Administrator Emil gives Karnos the advanced
  shadow weapon.
- The Karnos Mace and Two-Handed Axe get the part of their kRO bonus the
  server was missing, and the Karnos shadow weapon gets its set bonus with
  the five Advanced Paradise shadow pieces.

## 0.3.5
- New setting, "Refine tickets from champion hunts" (off by default). With
  it on, the Lv130 and Lv140 champion hunts on the Champion Hunt Board also
  give a +7 Weapon Refine Ticket and a +7 Armor Refine Ticket. Zerenia in
  the Paramarket trades these up, so her refine ticket trades no longer
  need tickets you could only get from the cash shop. This reward is the
  mod's own addition, not kRO's.

## 0.3.4
- Needs app 1.5.9 or newer.
- Paramarket trades paid in items open a barter window again, showing each
  price in the item you pay with: Malangdo Cans at Whiteberry and
  Blackphone, Einbech stones at Seth, dragon orbs at Earl, coins at Peary
  and refine tickets at Zerenia.

## 0.3.3
- Works on the current app again. 0.3.1 and 0.3.2 needed an app update
  that isn't out yet, so the Paramarket's item trades (Whiteberry,
  Blackphone, Seth, Earl, Peary and Zerenia's tickets) are back to the
  0.3.0 shop window. That window still shows their prices as Zeny.
- Keeps the shorter mission names on the mission boards from 0.3.2.

## 0.3.2
- Long mission names on the mission boards no longer spill onto a second
  line and cover the next mission. The board list shows a shorter name;
  the mission page and your quest log still show the full title.

## 0.3.1
- Paramarket trades paid in items now open a barter window that shows each
  price in the item you pay with: Malangdo Cans at Whiteberry and
  Blackphone, Einbech stones at Seth, dragon orbs at Earl, coins at Peary
  and refine tickets at Zerenia. Before, the shop window showed those
  prices as Zeny and your total as points.

## 0.3.0
- Nyang Time is back on the Eden Group's 2nd floor. She turns premium
  consumables (life and mana potions, Almighty, boosters and more) into True
  versions that last an hour.
- New optional setting: premium consumables can drop from any monster, at a
  0.1% chance per kill by default. You can change the chance in the mod's
  settings.
- The Paramarket is now kRO's current market: every shop is open every day,
  with new shops for Einbech stones, refine tickets, dragon orbs, coin
  exchange, MVP loot, Illusion gear, butterfly wings and Malangdo boxes.
  Prices follow kRO's but aren't confirmed yet.

## 0.2.9
- Quest tracker names now match the names in the quest text, for both
  monsters and hunting areas.
- Mission board titles now match the titles in your quest log.
- The champion board reads more clearly, and its NPC is now Diligent Sogil.
- New setting: Eden Coin multiplier (1 to 100, default 1) for mission
  rewards.
- Paramarket is entered through a portal in the Eden Group hall, as in kRO,
  with a Paramarket sign above it.
- Odin's Past can now be reached from Odin Temple 3, with a portal back.
- Office Assistant Neede's emote now shows over Secretary Lime Evenor.
- Clearer "report to" lines on mission NPCs, and typo fixes in Eden dialog.

## 0.2.8
- The Paradise Cap, Paradise Hat and their Advanced versions now show on
  your character.

## 0.2.7
- The Eden Group Mark now takes you to the Eden Group's entrance hall.
- Secretary Lime Evenor no longer says the Mark returns you to your save
  point; she says it brings you back to the Eden Group, once every 20 minutes.

## 0.2.6

## 0.2.5

  as kRO has them.

## 0.2.4

## 0.2.3

## 0.2.2

## 0.2.1

## 0.2.0
